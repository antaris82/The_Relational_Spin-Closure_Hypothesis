## 1. Eilenberg, Samuel; Mac Lane, Saunders — *Acyclic Models*

* Edition/version: American Journal of Mathematics **75** (1953), no. 1, pp. 189–199.
* Identifier: DOI `10.2307/2372628` (bibliographic record verified via the Crossref API,
  2026-09-09).
* Exact part used: the acyclic-models theorem itself (freeness of the domain functor on models,
  acyclicity of the codomain functor on models; extension and uniqueness-up-to-homotopy).
* Role: WP1 — the theorem whose hypotheses are instantiated in this audit.
* Influence: `THEOREM_STATEMENT_REFERENCE`.

## 2. Rotman, Joseph J. — *An Introduction to Algebraic Topology*

* Edition: Springer-Verlag, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 119, 1988. ISBN 0-387-96678-1.
* Exact part used: chapter 9, Theorem 9.12 (textbook form of the acyclic-models theorem).
* Verification status: the section/theorem number is taken from the citation given in the
  Wikipedia article *Acyclic model* (revision retrieved 2026-09-09); the book itself was not
  consulted in this run.
* Role: WP1 — the concrete textbook formulation of "free on models"/"acyclic on models".
* Influence: `THEOREM_STATEMENT_REFERENCE`.

## 3. Barr, Michael — *Acyclic Models*

* Editions: (a) Canadian Journal of Mathematics **48** (1996), no. 2, pp. 258–273,
  DOI `10.4153/CJM-1996-013-x`; (b) CRM Monograph Series **17**, American Mathematical Society,
  2002, ISBN 978-0821828779.
* Exact part used: the general framework (acyclic classes, presentable/free functors), and the
  observation that the simplicial/singular comparison there still passes through simplicial
  subdivision.
* Source of the bibliographic data: nLab page *acyclic object* (references section) and the
  Wikipedia article *Acyclic model*, both retrieved 2026-09-09.
* Role: WP1 (variants of the theorem), WP10 (dependency structure of the classical proofs).
* Influence: `THEOREM_STATEMENT_REFERENCE`.

## 4. Wikipedia — *Acyclic model*

* Version: revision id 1321158231 (page last modified 2025-11-09), retrieved 2026-09-09.
* URL: `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acyclic_model`
* Exact part used: the statement of freeness/acyclicity hypotheses, the pointer to
  Eilenberg–MacLane 1953, Rotman Thm. 9.12 and Barr, and the remark that the singular-chain
  application "uses a detour through simplicial subdivision".
* Role: WP1/WP10 cross-check of the hypothesis shape and of the literature pointers.
* Influence: `BACKGROUND_ONLY`.

## 5. Hatcher, Allen — *Algebraic Topology*

* Edition: Cambridge University Press, 2002; author's official electronic edition
  `https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/AT.pdf` (downloaded and read 2026-09-09).
* Exact part used: §2.1, **Theorem 2.27** ("the homomorphisms `H_n^Δ(X,A) → H_n(X,A)` are
  isomorphisms for all `n` and all Δ-complex pairs `(X,A)`") together with its proof on
  pp. 128–130: skeletal induction; the characteristic-map computation of `H_n(X^k, X^{k-1})`;
  Example 2.23 (`H_k(Δ^k, ∂Δ^k)` generated by the identity map); the five lemma; the
  compact-support step ("a compact set in `X` can meet only finitely many open simplices"); and
  the relative case by the five lemma on the two long exact sequences.
* Verification status: text extracted from the downloaded PDF and read verbatim; the proof
  contains no occurrence of "acyclic models".
* Role: WP10 (actual dependency structure of the classical comparison proof), WP11 (Route C and
  the compact-support step), Task-13 DAG.
* Influence: `PROOF_ROUTE_REFERENCE`.

## 6. Milnor, John — *The Geometric Realization of a Semi-Simplicial Complex*

* Edition: Annals of Mathematics **65** (1957), no. 2, pp. 357–362; DOI `10.2307/1969967`
  (bibliographic record verified via the Crossref API, 2026-09-09).
* Exact part used: the realisation of a simplicial set is a CW complex whose cells correspond to
  the nondegenerate simplices — the input that transports Hatcher's Δ-complex argument to
  realisations of simplicial sets, and the basis of the compact-support statement of WP6.
* Role: WP6, WP10, WP11.
* Influence: `PROOF_ROUTE_REFERENCE`.

## 7. May, J. Peter — *Simplicial Objects in Algebraic Topology*

* Edition: Van Nostrand Mathematical Studies 11, 1967 (reprinted, University of Chicago Press,
  1992).
* Exact part used: the CW structure of `|K|` and the identification of the normalised simplicial
  chain complex with the cellular chain complex of `|K|` (the cellular route to the comparison).
* Verification status: cited from standard knowledge; not re-verified online in this run.
* Role: WP10 (alternative architecture of the classical proof).
* Influence: `BACKGROUND_ONLY`.
