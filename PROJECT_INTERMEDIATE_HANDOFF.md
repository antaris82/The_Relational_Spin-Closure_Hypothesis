### What is the strongest regular-solder theorem?

Two, jointly:

* **identification** — a regular solder exists exactly when the internal Lorentz bundle is
  equivalent to `TM` (fibrewise linear, over the identity, continuous total-space maps, `C^∞`
  local representatives) [CLAIM-G008].  A regular smooth solder additionally gives smooth
  local/projected Lorentz transition representatives, so the missing stronger total-space smooth
  packaging is an **infrastructure / packaging limitation**, not a consequence of the native
  Spin cocycle being continuous; and that smooth projected Lorentz data is **not** a packaged
  smooth intrinsic Spin Lie-group bundle theory [CLAIM-B001];
* **geometry** — a regular solder induces a `C^∞` tangent metric that is symmetric,
  nondegenerate, tensorial under the genuine tangent transitions, and of signature (1,3) in
  every solder frame [CLAIM-G009].

Both are conditional on a solder being given.  Existence is a gate, not a theorem
[CLAIM-B007].

### What does Task 38 **not** establish?

It says nothing about other base gluings, nothing about co-varying bases, nothing about
transition deformations in general, and nothing whatsoever about curvature — no curvature object
exists [CLAIM-B003].  The tested family is the **selected** one-parameter subgroup generated by
the chosen Clifford direction `cle 0` [CLAIM-L010]: it is not the unique or canonical native
Spin deformation, and the result classifies neither the full native Spin deformation space nor
every possible native Spin deformation.  The Spin-side gauge is continuous, not smooth
[CLAIM-B001].  The control base is not a proved `S¹ × ℝ³` [CLAIM-B004].  The parameter is not a curvature and its sign is
not a curvature sign.

### Why is the present phase being closed here?

Because the first deformation knob the project was able to build turned out, **on the one
control where it could be tested exactly**, to be absorbable by full-Spin Čech gauge, with the
induced tangent Lorentz metric preserved under the certified solder-solution correspondence
[CLAIM-S004], [CLAIM-S010], [CLAIM-S011].  That is a statement about the tested family on the
frozen periodic fixed-cover control base and about nothing else: a general Čech-deformation
classification is **not proved** (`OP-007`, `OP-008`).  Continuing to push at the
Čech-transition layer would add scope without adding a new observable.  The honest move is to
freeze a reviewable intermediate state — mathematics, claims, scope limits, controls,
provenance and verification — and to start the next question in a new phase.

## The frozen intermediate conclusion

The present phase reconstructs an intrinsic Lorentz/Clifford/Spin core from the chosen Euclidean
three-dimensional starting datum, embeds that core in a global smooth four-dimensional manifold
architecture, and identifies the regular solder as the decisive bridge to actual tangent Lorentz
geometry.

In the regular-solder sector, project-native orientation and Spin compatibility conditions
re-emerge as global closure constraints rather than primitive local assumptions.

A first native one-parameter Spin Čech-transition deformation was then tested on the frozen
periodic control geometry.  The tested family is globally admissible for all finite parameter
values and is absorbable by full-Spin Čech gauge on that control.  On that control the regular
solder solution spaces at any two parameter values correspond, and corresponding solutions
induce exactly the same tangent Lorentz metric; in particular no parameter-dependent tangent
Lorentz metric remains.

Therefore, on that control, the first tested transition-function level is not yet the desired
dynamical/geometric deformation variable.  No claim is made about Čech transition deformations
in general.

The project closes this phase before introducing a connection, parallel transport, holonomy or
curvature layer.
